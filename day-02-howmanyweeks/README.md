# Day 2 · How Many Weeks

Your life in weeks: how many are behind you, how many are ahead, and what can still fit into them (summers, books, days with your parents, the things you want to finish).

- Single HTML file, no build step, no API key.
- Everything runs in the browser. Date, moments and goals are stored in `localStorage` on the device only.
- English by default, French available.

Sources: Tim Urban ("Your Life in Weeks", "The Tail End"), Paul Janet (1877), Wang & Peterson (2014), Google Books estimate (2010), Seneca.

Part of [The Unfinished 30](https://the-unfinished-30.vercel.app/) by [Salah Khenissi](https://www.linkedin.com/in/salahkhenissi/).
