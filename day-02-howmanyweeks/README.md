# Day 2 · How Many Weeks

Your life in weeks: how many are behind you, how many are ahead, and what can still fit into them.

- One HTML file, no build step, no API key. Download it and open it in a browser.
- Everything you type stays in the browser (`localStorage`). Export and import a JSON file to move between devices.
- The hosted page counts anonymous visits with Vercel Web Analytics (no cookies).
- English by default, French available.

What is inside: life grid with milestones, country and sex life expectancy (UN WPP 2024), "the times" (summers, books, parents, calls, grandparents, friends, date nights, custom), "Your people" (friends, children and family bars, couple, work: working days with holidays and days off, meetings, commute, screens, projects), goals (shared or not), share cards in five themes and two formats, native share, calendar reminders (.ics), keyboard-navigable grid.

Sources: Tim Urban ("Your Life in Weeks", "The Tail End"), Paul Janet (1877), Wang & Peterson (2014), Google Books estimate (2010), United Nations World Population Prospects 2024, Seneca.

Part of [The Unfinished 30](https://the-unfinished-30.vercel.app/) by [Salah Khenissi](https://www.linkedin.com/in/salahkhenissi/).
