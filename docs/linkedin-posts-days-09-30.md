## Day 9 · ZenFlow (Sun 29 Nov)

### Post
Most meditation apps want a subscription, a 14-question onboarding quiz and a daily streak before letting you take one slow breath.

Between back-to-back meetings, I don't need a 45-minute retreat. I need two minutes to drop my shoulders before the next call.

So for Day 9 of The Unfinished 30, I built **ZenFlow**.

Pick a duration (2 to 15 minutes) and a breathing rhythm — Box Breathing for focus, 4-7-8 for calm, or the Physiological Sigh for a quick reset. Mix offline Web Audio soundscapes (singing bowl, warm drone, soft rain) and log one line afterward if you want to remember what shifted.

Free, no account, runs in one HTML file. Nothing leaves your browser unless you plug in your own AI key for a custom guided script.
