# Day 25 · Odyssey 1-6-4

A minimalist Zen life & quarter architecture workspace built around the **1-6-4 framework**:

- **1 Annual North Star** — one guiding sentence + why it matters right now.
- **6 Quarterly Milestones** — one concrete outcome across Work, Health, Family, Craft, Finance, and Rest with 0%–100% progress steps.
- **4 Weekly Non-Negotiables** — four small rhythms with interactive Mon–Sun check dots.
- **Live Balance Radar & Bars** — an instant 6-axis SVG radar showing the real-time shape of your quarter.
- **Weekly Reflection Journal** — "What gave energy?" / "What drained it?", clarity score (1–5), and local weekly snapshots.
- **Print-ready 1-page A4 sheet** (`window.print()`) plus JSON Export/Import, localStorage autosave, EN/FR toggle, and standalone HTML download.

## Still unfinished

- Data lives exclusively in browser `localStorage` (no cloud sync or multi-device accounts yet; use Export JSON to back up).
- Quarter switching updates the label on the same 6 milestone slots rather than archiving separate per-quarter databases.
- No automated Sunday reminders or calendar integration.
