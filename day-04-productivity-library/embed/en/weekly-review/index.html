Weekly Review

A fixed weekly session that keeps the other systems honest. Without it, lists go stale.

Expected result

A refreshed calendar, action list and protected priorities for the week.

Fictional worked case

Six notes and two files remain open on Friday, while a key appointment is still unconfirmed.

Process the six notes, confirm the appointment and schedule the two follow-ups. Then reserve Tuesday for the most important work left.

Clear six notes and follow up on two files before reserving Tuesday morning for the next priority.

Steps

  1. Pick the same slot each week. Friday afternoon works for many people.
  2. Clear inboxes and capture loose items.
  3. Read last week's list: what got done, what did not, and why.
  4. Choose three priorities for next week and block them in the calendar.

Review

Start Monday with no unexplained note and a protected block for the next priority.

Limitations

Review stale projects and the coming calendar before promising new work.
