Retrospective

Reflect on what happened, identify insights and commit to improvements as a team.

Expected result

A single improvement experiment for the next work cycle.

Fictional worked case

The release passed tests early but still waited two days for approval.

Early testing worked, but the release still waited two days for approval. Book approval before the next release starts and compare the waiting time.

Early testing helped. Book the approval slot before the next launch and check whether the delay shrinks.

Steps

  1. Choose a bounded period or project.
  2. Capture facts and perspectives before debating.
  3. Agree one or two improvements with named owners.

Review

Compare approval waiting time after booking the next review before the release starts.

Limitations

Separate observable events from opinions and give the chosen action a review date.

Norm Kerth, Project Retrospectives: A Handbook for Team Reviews (2001).
