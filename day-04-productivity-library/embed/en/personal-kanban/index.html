Personal Kanban

Visualise your personal workload and limit work in progress rather than starting everything.

Expected result

A visible board with a limit on simultaneous work.

Fictional worked case

A freelancer has a quote, a contract and an invoice open at the same time. Switching is delaying all three.

The contract is in review and the invoice is ready to send. Finish one before starting the quote so work moves through the board.

Finish reviewing the contract before pulling more work if the in-progress limit is reached.

Steps

  1. Put all current tasks on cards.
  2. Choose a maximum of two active tasks.
  3. Move cards to Done before pulling new work.

Review

Keep no more than two active items and compare how many finish by Friday.

Limitations

Blocked work still counts against the limit. Make the blockage visible before pulling new work.

Jim Benson and Tonianne DeMaria Barry, Personal Kanban (2011).
