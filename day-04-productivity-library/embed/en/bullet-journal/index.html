Bullet Journal

Use rapid logging and intentional review to keep notes, plans and tasks in one place.

Expected result

A log that separates today’s notes from future commitments.

Fictional worked case

A student records ideas and deadlines in several notebooks and misses a renewal date.

Capture the topic idea as a note. Keep the draft review as a task. Move the renewal to the future log so it is easy to find when its month arrives.

Record the idea quickly. Move dated commitments to the appropriate log at review time.

Steps

  1. Capture tasks, events and notes using short symbols.
  2. Review and migrate unfinished items.
  3. Keep a monthly and future log for what matters later.

Review

At the next review, find the renewal and assignment deadlines in under one minute.

Limitations

Migrate only open items you still intend to act on. Copying every note makes the review harder.

Ryder Carroll, The Bullet Journal Method (2018).
