Deep Work

Protects long stretches for work that needs full concentration, and moves shallow tasks elsewhere.

Expected result

Protected calendar blocks tied to specific deliverables.

Fictional worked case

An analyst needs to validate a model and interpret its results before replying to routine requests.

Use the first block for a reproducible model test and the second to write the interpretation. Routine requests wait until the focused output exists.

Test and interpret the model in protected blocks. Answer messages after the second block.

Steps

  1. Choose one block of 60 to 90 minutes a day for your hardest work.
  2. Turn off notifications and close unrelated tabs.
  3. Before you stop, write the next step on paper. It makes restarting faster.

Review

Check whether both focus blocks produced a reproducible test and a written interpretation.

Limitations

Agree on an urgent contact route before turning off routine notifications.

Cal Newport, Deep Work (2016).
