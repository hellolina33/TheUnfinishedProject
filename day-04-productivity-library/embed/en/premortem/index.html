Pre-Mortem

Asks the team to assume failure in advance, which brings out risks people would hesitate to raise.

Expected result

A list of likely failure causes with preventive actions and owners.

Fictional worked case

A release has a fixed launch date, but nobody has booked testing or confirmed approval availability.

Assume the launch slipped. Late testing and unclear approval explain plausible paths to failure. Book testing Tuesday and have someone confirm the approver is available.

If approval risk could delay launch, book Tuesday testing now and assign someone to confirm it.

Steps

  1. Imagine it is a year from now and the decision failed.
  2. Write three reasons it failed.
  3. For each reason, decide one action that lowers the risk.

Review

Confirm the Tuesday test slot and the person who will sign off before the launch date.

Limitations

Start with independent notes so the first suggestion does not anchor the whole group.

Gary Klein, Harvard Business Review (2007).
