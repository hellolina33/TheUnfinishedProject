RICE Scoring

Adds reach and effort to ICE, so a popular but expensive feature does not win by default.

Expected result

A ranked backlog with assumptions you can challenge.

Fictional worked case

A team has room for one feature this quarter. It estimates reach over the same quarter and effort in person-months.

Feature A scores 400 × 2 × 0.8 ÷ 4 = 160. Feature B scores 800 × 1 × 0.5 ÷ 5 = 80. A reaches fewer people, but the estimated impact per person and confidence make it rank higher.

Rank feature A first for this quarter. Validate the reach estimate and the four person-months of effort before committing.

Steps

  1. Estimate Reach: how many people it touches in a set period.
  2. Score Impact, Confidence (as a percentage) and Effort in person-weeks.
  3. Calculate Reach × Impact × Confidence ÷ Effort. Sort the list by the result.

Review

Recheck the reach and confidence assumptions before committing to the 160-point option.

Limitations

Keep reach periods and effort units consistent. Recalculate with a lower confidence value if the evidence is weak.
