Don’t Break the Chain

A visible streak makes a daily habit harder to skip, because the gap shows on the calendar.

Expected result

A short record of practice days and a recovery plan.

Fictional worked case

A learner wants to practise every day. Travel makes one day likely to slip.

Mark the travel day as missed and resume the next day. The gap reveals when the normal practice setup was unavailable.

One missed day is visible. Resume the next day and look for what caused the gap.

Steps

  1. Choose one small daily action.
  2. Mark a cross on a calendar each day you do it.
  3. If you miss a day, restart the next day. Never miss twice in a row.

Review

Check whether practice resumes after a missed day and identify the obstacle.

Limitations

Track the action that matters. Keep missed days visible so you can diagnose the pattern.
