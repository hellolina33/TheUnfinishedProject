Start, Stop, Continue

Turns feedback into three kinds of action: what to begin, what to end, and what to keep.

Expected result

One behaviour to start, one to stop and one to keep.

Fictional worked case

Two people write the same meeting minutes. The written follow-up is useful, but the meeting often starts without an agenda.

The written follow-up is useful, so keep it. Two sets of minutes are redundant, so stop one. Try sending an agenda before the next meeting.

Keep the written follow-up and remove duplicate minutes. Test a short agenda next time.

Steps

  1. Write three lists for the last week: start, stop, continue.
  2. Pick one item from Start and one from Stop.
  3. Test them next week, and note the result.

Review

Check at the next meeting whether one agenda and one record were enough.

Limitations

Choose changes small enough to test by the next check-in.

No verified single author. Common in agile team retrospectives.
