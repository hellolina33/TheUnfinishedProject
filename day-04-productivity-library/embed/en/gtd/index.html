Getting Things Done (GTD)

Empties your head into one trusted list, then decides what each item needs next.

Expected result

A trusted list of next actions, reminders and reference material.

Fictional worked case

Supplier commitments are scattered across inbox threads. One quote needs a follow-up and one invoice only needs filing.

Requesting a quote is an action. The invoice is reference material. Once the request goes out, add a follow-up to the weekly review instead of leaving the thread unread.

Turn the supplier thread into a concrete next action. Keep its invoice as reference and review the follow-up on Friday.

Steps

  1. Write everything on your mind into one inbox, on paper or in an app.
  2. Process the inbox. Not actionable goes to trash, someday or reference.
  3. If it takes under two minutes, do it now. Otherwise schedule it or delegate it.
  4. Review all lists every week.

Review

At Friday’s review, verify that every open supplier thread has a dated next action.

Limitations

Write a physical or observable next action such as “email the supplier,” rather than a project name.

David Allen, Getting Things Done (2001).
