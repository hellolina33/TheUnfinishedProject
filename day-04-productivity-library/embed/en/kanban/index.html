Kanban Board

Shows all work as cards in columns. A limit on Doing stops you starting ten things at once.

Expected result

A shared view of the queue and its bottleneck.

Fictional worked case

Refund requests build up at finance approval. Taking more requests does not move the queue.

Refunds waiting at finance approval are older than new requests. Help finance clear the approval queue before increasing intake.

If refunds pile up at finance approval, look at that step before accepting more requests.

Steps

  1. Draw three columns: To do, Doing, Done.
  2. Write each task on a card in To do.
  3. Move one card to Doing. Keep Doing to two cards at most.

Review

Review the age of pending refunds before and after helping finance clear the queue.

Limitations

Look at the age and movement of work, not just the number of cards in each column.

David J. Anderson, Kanban (2010). Roots in the Toyota Production System (Taiichi Ohno).
