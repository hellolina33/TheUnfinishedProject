PDCA

Improve a process through short cycles of planning, testing, learning and adjustment.

Expected result

A measured trial and a decision to adopt, adjust or stop.

Fictional worked case

A support team wants to reduce a 48-hour median delay with a small change to request handling.

The baseline median was 48 hours. The ten-request pilot recorded 29 hours. Repeat with a comparable group before treating the apparent improvement as durable.

Compare the pilot median of 29 hours with the baseline of 48. Extend the trial before making a permanent change.

Steps

  1. Plan one small process change.
  2. Try it with a measurable result.
  3. Check the outcome and adopt or adjust the process.

Review

Repeat the pilot on a comparable group and compare medians before standardising.

Limitations

Decide how you will compare results before the trial. Keep the comparison conditions similar.

Shewhart cycle, popularized by W. Edwards Deming; often called PDCA.
