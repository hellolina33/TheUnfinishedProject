Shutdown Ritual

End each workday with a consistent review and a clear transition out of work.

Expected result

A short handoff to yourself for tomorrow morning.

Fictional worked case

A supplier quote and two emails stay on someone’s mind after the laptop closes.

The quote follow-up becomes a dated task and the 9 am call is tomorrow’s first action. The laptop can close once open commitments have somewhere to go.

Write down the quote follow-up and the 9 am call before closing the laptop.

Steps

  1. Capture unfinished work and stray thoughts.
  2. Identify tomorrow's first priority.
  3. Close work apps and use a consistent sign-off cue.

Review

Start at 9 am with the planned call and check whether any open commitment lacked a next action.

Limitations

Cap the shutdown ritual. Capture open work instead of starting another full work session.

Cal Newport, Deep Work (2016).
