SWOT Analysis

Separate internal strengths and weaknesses from external opportunities and threats.

Expected result

A short strategy that connects internal facts to external conditions.

Fictional worked case

A small team sees rising demand and stronger competitors but has a limited launch budget.

An experienced team can meet rising demand, but the budget constrains scope. A focused offer uses the strength while limiting exposure to stronger rivals.

Growing demand meets an experienced team, but the budget is limited. Start with a narrowly scoped offer.

Steps

  1. Define the decision or strategy you are examining.
  2. List strengths, weaknesses, opportunities and threats.
  3. Translate the strongest insights into actions.

Review

Test the narrow offer with five prospects before expanding its scope.

Limitations

Turn each useful combination into a choice. A long list of adjectives rarely guides action.
