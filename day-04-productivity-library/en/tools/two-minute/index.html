Two-Minute Rule

Clears small tasks as they come, so they stop waiting in a list where they grow.

Expected result

A cleared micro-task and a scheduled block for the larger work.

Fictional worked case

A room reservation needs a yes-or-no reply. Preparation for the same meeting will take half an hour.

A one-minute yes reserves the room. Preparing the meeting needs half an hour, so it goes on the calendar as a separate action.

Confirming the room takes one minute. Put the preparation itself on the calendar.

Steps

  1. When a task appears, estimate how long it takes.
  2. Under two minutes: do it at once.
  3. Over two minutes: put it on the list or in the calendar.

Review

Send the confirmation during triage and reserve a separate block for preparation.

Limitations

Use the rule while processing a queue. Interrupting focused work for every small request defeats the purpose.

David Allen, Getting Things Done (2001).
