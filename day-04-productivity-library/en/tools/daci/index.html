DACI

Clarify who drives a decision, who approves it and who contributes or is informed.

Expected result

A decision brief with one approver and a communication plan.

Fictional worked case

A supplier decision has several contributors and no clear person responsible for making the final call.

Sam gathers vendor inputs from Noor and prepares the options. Lea makes the call by the deadline, and the team receives the decision and its rationale.

Sam coordinates the supplier choice and Lea makes the final decision.

Steps

  1. Write the decision in one sentence.
  2. Name one Driver and one Approver.
  3. Add Contributors and Informed, then publish the decision date.

Review

Confirm that Lea decides by the deadline and that the team receives the rationale.

Limitations

Distinguish the person moving the decision forward from the person authorised to decide.
