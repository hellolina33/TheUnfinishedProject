10/10/10

Test a decision against three time horizons to counter short-term emotional bias.

Expected result

A written comparison across three time horizons.

Fictional worked case

An attractive side project would take capacity from a more important commitment for most of the year.

Saying no feels awkward immediately. Over ten months it frees the main team. In ten years the side project itself may carry little weight. That makes the short-term discomfort easier to weigh.

Saying no feels awkward today, but frees the team for the main project over the next ten months.

Steps

  1. Write the decision and two real alternatives.
  2. Imagine the consequences after 10 minutes, 10 months and 10 years.
  3. Choose the option that makes sense across timeframes.

Review

Record the trade-off now and revisit it when the main project reaches its next milestone.

Limitations

Write what could change the long-term judgment. A distant forecast is still uncertain.

Suzy Welch, 10-10-10: A Life-Transforming Idea (2009).
