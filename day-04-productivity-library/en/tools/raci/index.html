RACI Matrix

Makes explicit who does the work, who signs it off, who is asked, and who is kept informed.

Expected result

A responsibility matrix with one final approver per deliverable.

Fictional worked case

A contract sits unsigned because several colleagues think someone else owns the approval.

Lea drafts the contract and Sam approves it. Noor and Lina have other roles. Naming those roles stops everyone from waiting for someone else to sign.

Lea drafts the contract. Sam alone approves it, so the contract has a clear owner for sign-off.

Steps

  1. List the deliverables and the people involved, including you.
  2. For each deliverable, mark one R (does the work) and one A (signs it off).
  3. Mark C (asked) and I (informed) where needed. Check that every row has exactly one A.

Review

Confirm that each deliverable has one named approver and that the contract reaches Sam.

Limitations

Check for missing or duplicate approvers before distributing the table.
