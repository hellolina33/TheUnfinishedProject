After Action Review

Four questions asked straight after an event, to learn from what went differently from the plan.

Expected result

A record of the expected result, the actual result and a corrective action.

Fictional worked case

A noon delivery arrives at 2 pm. The team needs to understand the gap before repeating the same plan.

The team aimed for noon and delivered at 2 pm. Data arrived late. Requesting it the day before gives the team a change it can test on the next run.

Delivery slipped by two hours because the data arrived late. Request it the day before the next delivery.

Steps

  1. Write what you planned to happen, and what happened.
  2. Explain each difference in one sentence.
  3. Choose one thing to sustain and one thing to improve.

Review

Request data the day before and record the actual delivery time on the next run.

Limitations

Capture the expectation before explaining the gap. Focus on evidence and conditions rather than blame.
