Daily Highlight

Choose one meaningful achievement to protect in today's schedule.

Expected result

One protected calendar block and a visible result by day’s end.

Fictional worked case

A writer has several routine tasks but wants one concrete achievement by the end of the day.

Define success as a first page ready to review. Reserve 10 to 11 am and mute notifications during that block.

Define the highlight as a first page ready for review. Give it the 10 am slot.

Steps

  1. Ask which single thing would make today satisfying.
  2. Block protected time for it.
  3. At the end of the day, note what helped.

Review

At day’s end, check whether the first page is ready for someone else to review.

Limitations

Pick an outcome you can see or hand off. “Work on the report” makes completion ambiguous.

Jake Knapp and John Zeratsky, Make Time (2018).
