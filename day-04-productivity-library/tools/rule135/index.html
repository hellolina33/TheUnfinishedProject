1-3-5 Rule

Make a realistic daily list: one big task, three medium tasks and five small tasks.

Expected result

A daily plan with a protected main result and a bounded supporting list.

Fictional worked case

A freelancer has one workday before a proposal review and nine competing tasks.

The proposal is the big task. Review, pricing and scheduling are three supporting tasks. Five short messages fit only if the proposal block is secure.

Protect time for the proposal. Fit the smaller work around that commitment.

Steps

  1. Choose the single outcome that would make the day worthwhile.
  2. Add three important tasks that take less effort.
  3. Add five small tasks, then stop adding.

Review

At close, verify that the proposal is ready to review before counting the smaller tasks.

Limitations

On a meeting-heavy day, use fewer items. The numbers are limits you can adapt to your capacity.
