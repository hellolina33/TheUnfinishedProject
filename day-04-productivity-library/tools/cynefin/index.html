Cynefin Framework

Match your response to the type of situation instead of applying one decision process everywhere.

Expected result

A diagnosis of the situation and a matching first response.

Fictional worked case

A service desk handles a documented reset, a puzzling outage, a new service and an active incident.

A documented reset follows the procedure. An unusual outage needs expertise. A new service needs a small probe. A live incident needs containment before analysis.

Follow the documented reset procedure. Use a small experiment for a new service and contain a live incident immediately.

Steps

  1. Describe the situation without deciding too quickly.
  2. Ask whether cause and effect are obvious, analyzable, emergent or unstable.
  3. Choose best practice, expert analysis, safe-to-fail probes or immediate stabilization accordingly.

Review

Check that each issue receives a response appropriate to its uncertainty.

Limitations

Different parts of one problem can require different responses. Reclassify as you learn.

Dave Snowden; Snowden and Boone, 'A Leader's Framework for Decision Making', HBR (2007).
