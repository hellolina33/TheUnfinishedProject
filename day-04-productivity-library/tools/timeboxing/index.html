Timeboxing

Fixes the time you spend on something, not the result. When the box ends, you stop and decide.

Expected result

A bounded plan and a definition of a usable handoff.

Fictional worked case

A one-hour deadline leaves only enough time for an outline, a review and a usable handoff.

Spend 30 minutes drafting, 15 reviewing and 15 sending. At minute 30, switch to review if the essential points exist. Record any detail to revisit separately.

Stop drafting at 30 minutes. Use the remaining time to review and send a usable version.

Steps

  1. Decide how long the task gets before you start.
  2. Set a timer. Work until it rings.
  3. Then choose one: finish, extend once with a stated reason, or stop and log what is left.

Review

At minute 30, stop drafting and verify that the essential points are present.

Limitations

Define the minimum usable output before starting the timer.

James Martin, Rapid Application Development (1991). Used in the DSDM method.
