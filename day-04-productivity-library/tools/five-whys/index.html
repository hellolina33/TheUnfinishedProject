Five Whys

Trace a problem past its first explanation by asking 'why?' repeatedly, without assuming five is magic.

Expected result

A chain of plausible causes with a testable fix.

Fictional worked case

Three deliveries were late after approval requests arrived incomplete.

Delivery was late because approval started late. The requests lacked a required field. Compare old forms with completed requests before assigning someone to update the form.

Verify why the form is outdated before assigning an owner. Each step is a hypothesis to check.

Steps

  1. State the problem factually.
  2. Ask why, recording evidence rather than blame.
  3. Stop at a cause you can investigate or change.

Review

Check past requests for the missing field. Test the revised form on the next five deliveries.

Limitations

Follow the evidence and branch when several causes coexist. Five is a prompt, not a required number of answers.
