Impact–Effort Matrix

Puts ideas on two axes, so quick wins are visible at a glance and heavy work is weighed against its payoff.

Expected result

A short list of quick wins and larger bets to estimate.

Fictional worked case

A shop can do one small fix this week. A broken link blocks some buyers and a checkout redesign needs several weeks.

The broken link is quick to repair and blocks checkout. The larger redesign may help more buyers, but the team needs an effort estimate and evidence before scheduling it.

Repair the broken link now. Estimate the checkout redesign separately because it takes more work.

Steps

  1. Write each task on its own card.
  2. Place each card by Impact (up is high) and Effort (right is high).
  3. Do the quick wins now. Schedule the major projects. Drop the thankless tasks.

Review

Confirm the repaired link works and measure how often buyers still abandon that step.

Limitations

Compare impact using the same outcome and time window for every item.
