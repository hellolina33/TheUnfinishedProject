Time Blocking

Gives each type of work a fixed slot in the calendar, so tasks compete for time, not for your attention.

Expected result

A calendar that shows when work will happen and where delays fit.

Fictional worked case

A writer has missed two drafting sessions because calls and invoices filled the morning.

Reserve writing at 9 am. Put invoices and supplier calls into separate slots so they do not silently consume that block.

Protect writing at 9 am. Put calls and invoices in their own slots.

Steps

  1. Block the week: deep work, admin, meetings, breaks.
  2. Put each task into the block that matches it.
  3. When a block ends, stop, even if the task is not finished.

Review

Track whether the 9 am block actually happens three days in a row.

Limitations

Leave buffer between blocks and compare actual time with scheduled time for a week.

No single inventor. Widely described in productivity writing, for example Cal Newport, Deep Work (2016).
