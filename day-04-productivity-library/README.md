# Day 04 · Productivity Library

A bilingual library of 50 frameworks for prioritising, organising, focusing, deciding and reviewing. Each method has a worked example, a worksheet and an eight-slide presentation you can export to PowerPoint or PDF.

- `index.html` loads the studio, and `methods.js` holds the English and French catalogue.
- `studio.js` runs the catalogue and worksheets. `deck.js` builds the slide content and exports.
- `tools/` holds the share pages for individual methods.
- The studio’s **Download HTML** button bundles the app, catalogue and export libraries for offline use.
- Worksheet answers stay in the open tab. Download a backup to resume later.
