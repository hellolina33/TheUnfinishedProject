# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML, one self-contained file per page, no build step, deployed on Vercel. Each day of the challenge lives in its own folder. The tool must stay downloadable as a single HTML file that runs locally.

## Users

People who see Salah Khenissi's LinkedIn post for Day 2 of "The Unfinished 30" and open the link, mostly on a phone. Working adults, often parents, in France and internationally. They give it a minute out of curiosity, then some come back on Sunday evenings to colour the week.

## Product Purpose

How Many Weeks shows a whole life on one screen, one box per week, then counts what is left in the things that matter: summers with your kids, dinners with friends, calls with your parents, working days before retirement. The goal is not to scare. It is to help people choose where their weeks go. Success: a visitor enters a birth date within seconds, sees one number that stops them, and leaves with one thing to do (a Sunday check-in, a calendar event, a goal).

## Positioning

Not a memento mori poster. The weeks are counted in the units of a real life (summers, trips, dinners, days with a child before 18 with shared custody) and the grid becomes a weekly ritual: colour this week by what you gave it to. Free, no account, everything in the browser.

## Operating Context

Arrives from a LinkedIn post (link in the first comment). Read on a phone first. Used again on Sunday evenings, often with a printed A4/A3/A2 poster coloured by hand. Calendar files (.ics) and Google Calendar links carry the ritual out of the page.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Birth date (year alone is enough), lifespan slider or UN country/sex average, a 12-scene scroll story over a sticky grid (life, lived, left, rows, feel, times, circles: friends/family/couple/work, last times, other lives, choose goals, card), Sunday check-in with categories and tally, life chapters and milestones, moments with frequencies and calendar export, shareable card image, printable PDF poster, JSON export/import, EN and FR. Nothing leaves the browser except anonymous Vercel Web Analytics. No server, no account.

## Brand Commitments

Part of "The Unfinished 30" by Salah Khenissi: keep the challenge bar, the credit to Salah with his LinkedIn link, "Day 2/30", the honest "What is still unfinished" section and the "Download the HTML" link. Posts and page copy are in English with a full French version.

## Evidence on Hand

Salah's own numbers, public in his post, shown without names: 36 weeks of summer holidays left with his eldest before she turns 18 (3 weeks per summer, shared custody). Sourced figures in the tool: UN WPP 2024 life expectancy, INSEE/DREES averages, dated milestones of public figures with sources. No testimonials, no user counts, no press: none may be invented.

## Product Principles

- One number that lands, then one thing to do.
- Count in life units, not abstractions.
- Gentle, never morbid: the point is choice.
- Private by construction: nothing to sign up for, nothing sent.
- Honest about what is unfinished.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard navigation on the grid, colour categories always named in text, reduced motion respected, readable on a 360 px phone.
