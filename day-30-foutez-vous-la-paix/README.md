# Day 30 · Foutez-vous la Paix

The grand finale of **The Unfinished 30**: a serene, interactive digital reading experience for Salah Khenissi's *Foutez-vous la Paix* (original French voice with instant English edition toggle).

## Features
- **6 Thematic Chapters**: *01. Le bruit de la performance*, *02. Le piège de l'inachevé*, *03. Dire non sans s'excuser*, *04. La règle du assez bien*, *05. Reprendre son temps*, and *06. Épilogue : Jour 30/30*.
- **Calm Reading Controls**: Cream, Sepia, and Night reading themes, adjustable literary serif font size, chapter navigation, and a live reading progress bar.
- **Carnet de Paix**: Click any passage to highlight and save it locally (`localStorage`).
- **60-Second Breathing Pause**: Guided 4s inhale · 4s hold · 6s exhale breathing orb modal offered between chapters.
- **Shareable Quote Card PNG**: Turn any highlighted passage into a high-resolution 1080×1350 PNG quote card in 4 literary styles via HTML5 Canvas.
