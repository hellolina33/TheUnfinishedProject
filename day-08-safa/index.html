Flight mode

Paris to Bangkok.
When do I pray?

Departure, arrival, take-off time. safa. follows the plane minute by minute, reads the sun above it and gives you each prayer in both time zones.

Travelling. Most scholars allow shortening four-unit prayers to two (Qasr) and combining Dhuhr with Asr, Maghrib with Isha (Jam'). Conditions differ between schools. Ask someone of knowledge.

Estimate. Great-circle route, constant speed, sea-level horizon: at cruising altitude, sunset comes a few minutes later. Not a religious ruling.

Sources

Where the texts come from

Every verse and every dua shows its reference. You can report an error from the app.

  • Quran: Uthmani text (Hafs), Noble Quran Encyclopedia
  • Translations of the meaning: Talal Itani and Pickthall (English), Rashid Maash and Hamidullah (French)
  • Word by word: Quran.com and the Quranic Arabic Corpus
  • Explanation: Al-Mukhtasar (Tafsir Center), Al-Wahidi on the circumstances of revelation
  • Duas: Hisnul Muslim (Fortress of the Muslim) by Sa’id al-Qahtani
  • Times and Qibla: computed on your phone with adhan-js
  • Recitations: everyayah.com

What's unfinished

  • The app is an installable web app, not yet a native iOS or Android build. No home-screen widgets. No prayer notifications while it is closed: it exports calendar reminders instead.
  • Recitation and word audio stream from public servers. Saving a surah's audio for offline is built but still needs testing on real phones.
  • Dua source references are matched automatically for about half of Hisnul Muslim. All of them need review by a person of knowledge before a public release.
  • Translation and tafsir licences must be confirmed before any store release.
  • The phone on this page is a small preview. The full app is one tap away.
