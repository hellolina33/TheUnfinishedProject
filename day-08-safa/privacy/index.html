What safa. keeps, and what it never keeps

Short version: everything stays on your phone. No account, no server of ours, no tracker.

What is kept, and where

  • Your city, or your location if you share it once, to compute prayer times and the Qibla.
  • Your settings (language, method, theme).
  • What you choose to log: prayers ticked, reading, memorisation, make-up prayers, your personal duas.

Everything is stored in your browser, on your phone. Nothing is sent to safa.

What is never collected

  • No account, no e-mail, no name.
  • No usage analytics, no ad tracker, no data resale.
  • No fonts or scripts loaded from Google or any third party.

The only outside calls

Three public services are called when you play audio: everyayah.com for Quran recitations and hisnmuslim.com for dua audio, and audio.qurancdn.com for word audio. Like any web page, they see your IP address at that moment. If you save audio for offline use, these calls stop.

“Report an error” links open GitHub, only if you click them.

Opening Thai tafsir connects to QuranEnc.com for the selected verse. QuranEnc can see your IP address at that moment.

Backup and sync

The backup is a file you keep yourself. By default it is encrypted with your passphrase (AES-256). Without it, nobody can read it, not even safa.

Erase everything

Settings › “Erase everything on this device” deletes all your data. You can also clear the site data in your browser.
