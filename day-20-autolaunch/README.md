# Day 20 · AutoLaunch

A one-click SaaS & side-project launchpad for makers and freelancers that turns a short project brief into a complete launch kit — 100% locally in the browser.

## Features
- **3 Realistic Presets + Live Brief Editor**: Start immediately with *PulseClip*, *InvoiceNudge*, or *ShelfNote*, or type your own project name, 1-line pitch, target audience, pricing model, and tone.
- **Landing Page Blueprint**: Live styled preview with hero headline, subhead, 3 value props, objection-handling FAQ, and CTA — plus one-click **Copy HTML** (clean standalone snippet) and **Copy Copywriting**.
- **14-Day Launch Calendar**: Checkable daily tasks across LinkedIn, X, Reddit/IndieHackers, and DM Outreach, complete with channel filters and ready-to-copy tailored drafts for every day.
- **Ship-Ready Checklist**: Categorized pre-launch checklist covering OG image & social cards, analytics & mobile polish, pricing & checkout, and your first 5 users.
- **Markdown Export & Local Persistence**: Download your entire Launch Kit as a `.md` file; progress auto-saves in `localStorage`.
- **Optional BYOK AI Polish**: Bring your own Gemini, OpenAI, or Anthropic API key (stored only in browser `localStorage`) to rewrite the landing blueprint with AI.
- **Bilingual & Portable**: Full EN/FR toggle and one-click standalone HTML download.
