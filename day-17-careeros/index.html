What's still unfinished

CareerOS is a day-17 single-file prototype. It stores everything cleanly in your browser, with a few real limitations to keep in mind:

  • No email/calendar sync or browser push reminders—follow-up badges only alert you when you open the page.
  • Everything lives in browser localStorage: clearing site data wipes your board unless you hit "⬇ JSON" to back it up.
  • No automatic LinkedIn/ATS job-posting scraper; adding a new opportunity takes ~15 seconds of manual entry.
