# Day 1 · WhichOneAreYou

Drop a WhatsApp export and find out which role everyone really plays: the Firefighter, the Ghost, the Night Owl… 40 cards, 27 measured behaviours, each tied to a research paper.

Live: https://the-unfinished-30.vercel.app/day-01-whichoneareyou/

- Everything is computed in your browser. The chat is never uploaded.
- One unit of speech = a turn (messages less than 5 min apart). Rates are shrunk toward the group average so small samples don't win rankings. Reply times skip the night when the answer comes the next morning.
- Optional AI assistant: bring your own key (Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Grok). The key stays in your browser and goes straight to the provider you pick. Never commit a key.

Single file: `index.html`. Fork it, rebuild it, make it yours.
