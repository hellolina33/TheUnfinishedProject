# Day 5 · Rain Room

Rain, music and ambient sounds to work to. Pick a mood, mix your own room, set a focus timer, press play.

- One HTML file, no build step, no audio files: every sound is generated live with the Web Audio API.
- 18 ambient sounds in 5 groups, each with its own volume: rain, rain on a roof, thunder, wind, waves, stream, birds, night crickets, café, fireplace, night train, city, vinyl, clock, keyboard, white, pink and brown noise. Sounds are built only when you turn them up.
- 5 generative music styles, with their own volume: jazz trio, lo-fi beats, bossa nova, solo piano, ambient. New key and tempo every few choruses.
- 10 moods, a focus timer (25, 50, 90 min, custom, or Pomodoro 25/5 ×4) with a soft bell between phases and a fade-out at the end.
- Master volume, Space to play or pause, EN/FR. Your mix is saved in this browser only.

Part of [The Unfinished 30](https://the-unfinished-30.vercel.app/) by [Salah Khenissi](https://www.linkedin.com/in/salahkhenissi/).
