# Day 5 · Rain Room

A cassette player for deep work. Pick a tape, mix your room on the desk, set an auto stop, press play.

- One HTML file plus a `snd/` folder of short, looped recordings and instrument samples (about 7 MB, loaded only when you turn a sound up).
- 12 real recordings (rain, storm, wind, waves, stream, birds, crickets, café, fire, train, city, boat), looped seamlessly in the browser, plus 6 synthesized textures and noises.
- 5 music styles played live from real samples: jazz trio (Salamander grand piano, double bass, brushes), lo-fi, bossa nova (nylon guitar), solo piano, ambient. The program improvises, so it never repeats.
- Tape mode: wow and flutter, saturation, warm EQ and a little hiss on the music.
- Mixing desk with 19 faders, 10 tapes (moods), radio buttons for the music, LCD with VU meter, auto stop (25, 50, 90 min, custom, Pomodoro 25/5 x4), EN/FR, keyboard (Space, arrows).
