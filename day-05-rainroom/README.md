# Day 5 · Rain Room

Rain, soft jazz and noise to work to. Mix your own focus room, pick a mood, set a timer, press play.

- One HTML file, no build step, no audio files: every sound is generated live with the Web Audio API.
- Rain, thunder, fireplace, waves, vinyl, white, pink and brown noise are shaped noise.
- The jazz is a small generative trio: Rhodes-style FM piano comping and melody, walking bass, brushes. New key and tempo every few choruses.
- Six moods, a 25/50/90 min focus timer with fade-out, master volume, Space to play or pause, EN/FR.
- Your mix is saved in this browser only (localStorage).

Part of [The Unfinished 30](https://the-unfinished-30.vercel.app/) by [Salah Khenissi](https://www.linkedin.com/in/salahkhenissi/).
