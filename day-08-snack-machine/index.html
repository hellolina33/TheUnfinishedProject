What is still unfinished

YouTube only offers ready-made files up to 360p with sound; 720p and 1080p need the picture and sound stitched together, which the machine cannot do yet. YouTube and Instagram sometimes block cloud servers like this one, so some links get refused. TikTok and Instagram rarely publish a separate audio track: "audio" then gives you the smallest video. Long videos (over 45 minutes) and playlists are refused. The daily limits are best effort.
