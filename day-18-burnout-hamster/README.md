# Day 18 · Burnout: Le Hamster sous Stéroïdes

An interactive digital book chapter & self-reflection tool inspired by Salah Khenissi's writing on Stage I of burnout: uncontrolled over-ambition ("the hamster wheel on steroids").

## What it does
- **Editorial Book Experience**: Warm archival paper palette, serif typography, and a sticky animated SVG hamster-wheel speed indicator whose rotation responds live to your scroll progress and self-check score (with a pause-motion button).
- **4 Punchy Interactive Reading Sections (EN & FR)**: Short essays on *The Invisible Audition*, *The Moving Goalpost Reflex*, *Velocity Masquerading as Meaning*, and *Pulling the Micro-Brake*—each with an expandable private "Pause & reflect" prompt saved in `localStorage`.
- **2-Minute Wheel Speed Self-Check**: 8 honest sliders that calculate your current RPM zone (*Cruising* / *Overheating* / *Redline*) and prescribe 3 tailored, gentle micro-brakes to pull this week.
- **Downloadable Reflection Card (PNG)**: Generates a crisp editorial summary card locally on `<canvas>` with your zone, 3 micro-brakes, and personal weekly promise.
- **100% Private**: Zero analytics, zero servers—everything stays strictly in your browser.

## Still unfinished
- This is a self-reflection mirror, not a clinical diagnostic tool.
- Only Stage I of the burnout arc is written here; later stages (cynicism, quiet detachment, recovery) are not yet included.
- No multi-week trend history or reminder loop yet.
