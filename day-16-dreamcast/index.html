What's still unfinished

DreamCast is a single-file prototype built in one day for The Unfinished 30. It works completely offline, but a few magical touches are still missing:

  • Browser SpeechSynthesis voices vary wildly by OS (macOS & iOS sound warmest; some Linux browsers sound robotic). Real studio-grade neural voices would need a paid TTS API.
  • The offline story engine weaves modular scenes and dialogue, so after 10–15 nights you may recognize familiar story rhythms unless you plug in a BYOK AI key.
  • No real MP3 podcast export or background lock-screen audio session yet—keeping the screen dimmed works best for now.
