What's unfinished

  • Pitch-preserved WAV export uses a lightweight Hann-windowed overlap-add (OLA) time-stretcher in pure JS; extreme speeds (0.5x or 2.5x) on percussive tracks can sound slightly metallic compared to a full phase vocoder or RubberBand WASM.
  • Exports uncompressed 16-bit WAV only (no MP3/AAC encoder bundled) so files can be larger for long podcasts.
  • No waveform zoom, fade-in/fade-out curves, or multi-clip splicing yet.
